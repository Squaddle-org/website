The “we should totally…” list for your friend group.

Drop ideas into a shared room, vote on them, and turn the favorites into calendar events. Invite people with a link in any chat — no accounts, no passwords, no phone numbers. Everything is end-to-end encrypted, so the server never sees your plans, and you can self-host it if you like.

Make it real

Turn the winning idea into an event with one tap, and drop it straight into your phone’s calendar.

Nobody reads your plans. Not even us.

Every room is end-to-end encrypted on your devices before it ever reaches a server. Relays only ever see ciphertext — they route your room’s data without being able to read it. There's no sign-up, no email address, no phone number to leak. If you'd rather not trust anyone else's server at all, Squaddle's relay is open and simple enough to run yourself.
